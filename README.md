## Garage Restoration — $4.8 Million

### Project Overview
Restoration of the parking garage serving an occupied, 619-unit residential community spanning two 14-story towers.

### Operational Challenge
Coordinate construction phases while maintaining building operations, managing parking disruptions, and communicating changing access requirements to residents and staff.

### My Role
- Coordinated contractor activities with daily property operations.
- Supported construction phasing, parking arrangements, and traffic routing.
- Communicated work schedules and access changes to residents.
- Coordinated with management, engineering staff, and project stakeholders.
- Monitored operational concerns and supported resolution during construction.

### Outcome
The restoration was completed in 2026, with property operations maintained throughout phased construction.

### Lessons for Project Delivery
- Include operational access requirements in construction planning.
- Communicate upcoming changes before each phase.
- Maintain clear responsibilities between contractors and property staff.
- Address field conditions promptly with the appropriate project stakeholders.
